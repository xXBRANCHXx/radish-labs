THE OPERATING PROBLEM

A dispatch board is a machine for making promises under pressure.

The challenge is not displaying every data point. It is helping the operator see which promise is at risk, what can still be changed, and who needs to know—before the situation becomes an incident.

01 / PRIORITIZE

Urgency is computed, not colored in.

Mission priority combines safety, SLA exposure, customer impact, team fit, and travel conditions into one explainable order.

02 / COORDINATE

Every view shares one state.

Queue, map, mission detail, team capacity, and event feed update together so operators never reconcile five versions of the truth.

03 / RECOVER

The system looks for the next best move.

Route optimization is framed as a recoverability tool: protect the critical promise without quietly breaking three others.

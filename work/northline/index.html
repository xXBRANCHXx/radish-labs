THE PRODUCT PROBLEM
A configurable product has thousands of valid versions—and even more invalid ones.
A useful configurator cannot simply reveal options. It has to encode compatibility, preserve customer intent, explain tradeoffs, and translate the final choice into something production can act on.

        
01 / MODEL
One source of product truth.
Footprints, service modules, finishes, pricing, and lead-time impacts live in one rule model instead of separate sales documents.

        
02 / GUIDE
Constraints become guidance.
The interface prevents impossible combinations and explains why—without making the customer learn the underlying system.

        
03 / HANDOFF
The last click creates real work.
The generated specification gives sales, engineering, and production the same configuration code and decision record.

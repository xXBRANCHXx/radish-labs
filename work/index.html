Three kinds of build, one test: does it help someone do something that matters?

Websites / tools / automation
01

PUBLIC-FACING
SYSTEMS

When the website needs to explain, qualify, sell, schedule, collect, or connect — not just decorate.

Useful for

  • A new offer that needs a sharp launch
  • A business that has outgrown its template
  • A service that needs qualification or booking
  • A team that needs control of its content

Could include

  • Positioning and information architecture
  • Custom interface and interaction design
  • CMS, forms, calculators, or account areas
  • Performance, accessibility, and analytics
EXAMPLE BUILD / NOT A CASE STUDYA quote-ready service site

Visitor selects a service → answers useful questions → receives the right next step → the team gets a structured lead.

02

INTERNAL
OPERATING TOOLS

When spreadsheets, chat threads, and memory have quietly become the company software.

Useful for

  • Orders moving through several hands
  • Teams repeating the same admin every day
  • Clients who need a clear self-serve portal
  • Decisions trapped in scattered data

Could include

  • Dashboards and role-based workspaces
  • Inventory, CRM, or workflow systems
  • Client, partner, or staff portals
  • Reporting and operational alerts
EXAMPLE BUILD / NOT A CASE STUDYAn order control room

One source of truth for incoming orders, stock decisions, ownership, exceptions, and daily reporting.

03

AUTOMATION &
CONNECTIVE TISSUE

When the tools are fine but the gaps between them keep costing attention.

Useful for

  • Copying data between tools
  • Follow-ups that depend on someone remembering
  • Reports assembled by hand
  • AI ideas that need a real workflow around them

Could include

  • API and platform integrations
  • Rules, triggers, and approvals
  • Notifications and scheduled reporting
  • Purpose-built AI-assisted workflows
EXAMPLE BUILD / NOT A CASE STUDYA quiet handoff engine

Approved sale → clean project record → right people notified → client receives exactly what happens next.
